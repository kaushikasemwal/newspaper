The thing that actually matters here? India has the world's highest snakebite mortality, and we're still treating it like a footnote.

Global Gossip

Both sides signed because of US-Iran deal pressure, not because anyone actually wants peace. Two sides to this, obviously, but one of them has a much weaker argument.

Their early friendship has clearly frayed since the Iran situation, and the fact that both leaders are airing it publicly? That tells you everything about where the transatlantic vibe is right now.

Cuba just passed its biggest economic reforms since the 1960s revolution. Under intense US pressure, they're liberalizing significant chunks of their economy. But here's what nobody's asking — if these reforms actually work, does that validate decades of US sanctions as a strategy, or prove that Cuba could've done this on its own terms all along?
